Stuffed Baked Tomatoes

Sides

Ingredients

  • 6 medium tomatoes
  • 1 cup Italian bread crumb
  • 4 tablespoons grated Parmesan cheese
  • 4 tablespoons grated American or cheddar cheese
  • 4 tablespoons melted butter
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon freshly ground pepper
  • Chopped fresh parsley for garnish

Instructions

  1. Cut a thin slice off the top of each tomato. Scoop out pulp, leaving a 1/2-in. shell. Invert onto paper towels to drain. Mix stuffing ingredients. Spoon into tomatoes.
  2. Place tomatoes on a baking dish. Bake at 350° for 30 minutes. Last few minutes, broil until crusty brown. Keep a close eye on them, don't over do it…
