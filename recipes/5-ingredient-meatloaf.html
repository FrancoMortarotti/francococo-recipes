5-Ingredient Meatloaf (No Breadcrumbs)

Entree

This is such an easy recipe, and so quick to prepare. And with no breadcrumbs for those of you who are GMO intolerant makes this recipe a go to on most everyone's list.

Ingredients

  • 16 oz 80% Lean Ground Beef
  • 1 cup Parmesan Cheese, finely grated.
  • ½ cup Onion, diced
  • 1 Large Egg
  • ⅓ cup Ketchup ⅓ BBQ sauce for spreading on top of loaf
  • ½ teaspoon Salt
  • ½ teaspoon Ground Pepper

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 400 degrees F. Coat a loaf pan with olive oil
  2. Finely dice the Onion
  3. Mix all ingredients (except the ⅓ cup Ketchup ⅓ BBQ sauce reserved for the top) together in a large bowl with a fork or with hands: beef, parmesan cheese, onion, egg, salt, pepper.
  4. Form a loaf in the loaf pan. Spread the Ketchup/BBQ sauce over the top of the meatloaf. Bake in the oven for 45 minutes.
  5. Remove from the oven and let sit for 5 minutes. Remove from the pan, cut and serve with roast or mashed potatoes or salad. Enjoy!
